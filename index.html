HOW TO PLAY QIX

🎮 KEYBOARD CONTROLS

ARROWS or W A S D Move diamond marker
SPACE or J Fast Draw (Blue / Cyan - Normal Pts)
SHIFT or K Slow Draw (Red / Orange - 2X+ POINTS)
P Pause / Resume Game
M Toggle Sound (Mute)

📱 TOUCH CONTROLS

Use the on-screen D-Pad to steer along borders and into the field. Hold FAST DRAW or SLOW DRAW while pressing a direction to cut into territory.

🎯 OBJECTIVE & IMAGE UNCOVER

You control the diamond marker along the borders. Hold Fast Draw or Slow Draw and steer into the open area to draw lines (Stix). Connect back to any border to capture the territory and uncover the hidden background art! Claim 75% of the screen to advance.

⚡ FAST DRAW VS. SLOW DRAW

Fast Draw (Blue) draws quickly for safety, awarding standard points.
Slow Draw (Red/Orange) draws at half speed through high danger, but awards DOUBLE POINTS and rich vibrant luster!

🌀 THE QIX

The spinning geometric multi-line entity bounces unpredictably. You are safe while on borders, but if the Qix touches you or your uncompleted Stix while you are drawing, you lose a life!

🔥 THE FUSE

If you stop moving while drawing a Stix, a fuse ignites at the line's start and sizzles toward you! Move immediately to outrun it; if it reaches you, you lose a life.

✨ SPARX & SUPER SPARX

Red Sparx patrol the active borders. If one hits you, you lose a life. Watch the Sparx Meter: when time expires, an additional Sparx spawns and all Sparx mutate into Super Sparx that can pursue you down your Stix!

💥 DUAL QIX SPLIT (LEVEL 3+)

Starting at Level 3, two Qixes roam the field. If you draw a line that isolates the two Qixes into separate chambers, the level is immediately cleared and awards a permanent Score Multiplier (up to 9x) for all remaining levels!

🔐 CABINET PASSCODE MANAGEMENT

Authorized cabinet administrators can update the master security passcode. (Default: QIX1981)
